# EcoDash — Solar Medical Drone Simulator

**EcoDash** is a browser game where you pilot a solar-powered medical drone across rural Kenya to deliver life-saving supplies to remote villages.

Built with HTML5 Canvas, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No installation or complex setup required!

---

## The Story Behind the Game

- **The Problem:** In rural Kenya, bad roads and long distances make it extremely difficult for remote communities to get urgent medical supplies in time.
- **The Solution:** Solar-powered delivery drones that fly over tough terrain straight to village clinics.

---

## How to Play

1. **Fly the Drone:** Use **WASD** or the **Arrow Keys** to move.
2. **Recharge:** Keep an eye on your battery! Fly inside the golden solar zones to recharge.
3. **Avoid Hazards:**
   - **Trees & Huts:** Don't crash into them.
   - **Rivers:** Flying over water slows you down.
   - **Wildlife Zones:** Watch out for giraffe crossing areas.
4. **Pause:** Press **P** or **Esc** anytime.

## How the Files Work

- `index.html` — The main game screen and user interface.
- `style.css` — Colors, fonts, and hand-drawn sketch styling.
- `player.js` — Drone movement and battery logic.
- `obstacle.js` — Draws the trees, huts, wildlife signs, and rivers.
- `game.js` — Runs the game loop, collisions, and map generation.
- `main.js` — Handles button clicks, high scores, and menu screens.
- `audio.js` — Sound effects for collisions and impacts.
